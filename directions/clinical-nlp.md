# Clinical NLP

Clinical and biomedical text classification, transfer across corpora, and calibration beyond headline metrics.

**3 selected projects.**

## [Social and behavioural determinants from clinical text](https://github.com/terry0809000/MIMIC_SBDH)

How do conventional and transformer models compare for SBDH classification?

**Methods:** Clinical-text preparation, TF-IDF baselines, frozen embeddings, fine-tuning and benchmark evaluation.

**Data access:** Authorized MIMIC-III text / MIMIC-SBDH annotations

**Interpretation:** Access-controlled clinical text must remain in an authorized environment.

[Code and documentation](https://github.com/terry0809000/MIMIC_SBDH) · [Pipeline guide](../pipelines/MIMIC_SBDH.md)

## [Cross-corpus abstract classification](https://github.com/terry0809000/biomedical-abstract-sentence-classification)

How do lightweight sentence classifiers transfer between corpora?

**Methods:** Split-aware preprocessing, calibration and cross-corpus evaluation.

**Data access:** CPU · public text corpora

**Interpretation:** Read version-specific evidence; the local revision can be newer than the public release.

[Code and documentation](https://github.com/terry0809000/biomedical-abstract-sentence-classification) · [Pipeline guide](../pipelines/biomedical-abstract-sentence-classification.md)

## [Beyond Macro-F1](https://github.com/terry0809000/beyond-macro-f1-pubmed-rct-public)

Does better classification imply better calibration and perturbation consistency?

**Methods:** Sanitized benchmark code, result provenance and versioned evidence.

**Data access:** Public code + versioned evidence

**Interpretation:** A benchmark comparison does not establish clinical utility.
