## [Eating disorder dynamics across everyday moments](https://github.com/terry0809000/eatingdisordermlvar)

How do eating-related symptoms, affect and maladaptive exercise connect across EMA surveys?

**Methods:** R workflow comparing base and additive contemporaneous and temporal networks, with same-participant and same-day survey lags.

**Data access:** Authorized EMA CSV · R

**Interpretation:** Associational networks in exercise-target observations; not causal effects or individual diagnosis.
