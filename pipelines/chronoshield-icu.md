# ChronoShield-ICU count-matched temporal evaluation

![Conceptual scientific cover](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/terry0809000/chronoshield-icu/main/assets/project-cover.png)

Sometimes the time of a measurement tells us as much about the care process as the value itself. ChronoShield asks a narrower question: what remains in the timing signal once measurement counts are matched?

I preserve the cohort rules, availability views and staged review gates so that the comparison stays interpretable. The workflow begins with independently authorized raw archives and writes private outputs in a fresh workspace. It does not turn retrospective timing associations into a clinical deployment claim.

**Research direction:** Trustworthy AI

## Methods and inputs

Count-matched ICU temporal views, source-derived cohort construction and staged fixed-model evaluation.

Credentialed MIMIC-IV/eICU archives; keep extracted records and individual outputs private.

## Scope

Retrospective timing can reflect care processes; fresh extraction and model review gates are required.

Existing source was organized for publication; scientific experiments and tests were not rerun. Cover art is conceptual and does not depict measured results.
