# Healthcare affordability and testing gaps

![Conceptual scientific cover](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/terry0809000/nhis-affordability-testing-gaps/main/assets/project-cover.png)

A missed test can begin long before anyone reaches a clinic. I wanted to explore how the cost of care connects with gaps in testing, using public-use NHIS survey data.

The code brings together affordability, income and insurance profiles, service use and testing strategies. It keeps the analytical choices visible so you can follow the question from source tables to the final summaries. These are survey-based associations; they do not establish what caused a person to miss care.

**Research direction:** Health Informatics

## Methods and inputs

Public-use NHIS survey processing, affordability profiles and testing-gap analyses.

Public-use NHIS 2024/2025 source releases; obtain source tables separately.

## Scope

Survey-based associations do not establish causal effects on access to care.

Existing source was organized for publication; scientific experiments and tests were not rerun. Cover art is conceptual and does not depict measured results.
