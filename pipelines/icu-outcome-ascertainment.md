# Outcome ascertainment and transportability in ICU prediction

![Conceptual scientific cover](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/terry0809000/icu-outcome-ascertainment/main/assets/project-cover.png)

Before asking whether a model predicts an event, I want to know how that event became visible in the data. This project studies the gap between a recorded ICU endpoint, its timing and the clinical story it is meant to represent.

The saved code traces endpoint construction, observation windows, definition sensitivity and transportability across ICU archives. Later amendments and their required intermediates are documented in the pipeline guide. Agreement between implementations is kept separate from independent clinical adjudication, which was not performed.

**Research direction:** Trustworthy AI

## Methods and inputs

Recorded endpoint construction, observation timing, definition sensitivity and cross-database evaluation.

Credentialed ICU archives; source ascertainment and availability limitations are retained.

## Scope

Implementation agreement and recorded events do not constitute independent clinical adjudication.

Existing source was organized for publication; scientific experiments and tests were not rerun. Cover art is conceptual and does not depict measured results.
