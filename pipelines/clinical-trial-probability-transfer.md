# Probability quality in clinical-trial inference

![Conceptual scientific cover](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/terry0809000/clinical-trial-probability-transfer/main/assets/project-cover.png)

An answer and the confidence behind it are different things. I examine six small language models on clinical-trial inference, asking how probability quality and selective decisions behave when answer order changes.

The scoring contract distinguishes answer order, probability calibration and selective transfer. Component-aware uncertainty and sparse-bin warnings matter; undefined metrics must not be silently replaced with zero.

**Research direction:** Clinical NLP

**Also:** Trustworthy AI

## The question

How do six small language models differ in probability quality and selective transfer under paired answer-order changes?

## Methods and inputs

Frozen two-order scoring, development-fitted calibration, paired Brier contrasts and selective-risk diagnostics.

Acquire pinned SemEval-2024 Task 2 inputs and authorized model revisions separately.

[Open the source and pipeline →](https://github.com/terry0809000/clinical-trial-probability-transfer)

## Evidence boundary

Paired benchmark evidence does not establish clinical utility; exploratory post-outcome order policies do not replace the primary policy.

Existing study source was organized for this portfolio; scientific experiments and tests were not rerun. The AI-generated cover is conceptual raster artwork.
