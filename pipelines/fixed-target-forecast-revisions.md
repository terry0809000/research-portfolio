# Fixed-target forecast revisions under sensor outages

![Conceptual scientific cover](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/terry0809000/fixed-target-forecast-revisions/main/assets/project-cover.png)

A forecast can change even when the future it refers to stays the same. I wanted to understand what happens when sensor messages arrive late, and whether a revised prediction reflects new information or simply a changed representation of time.

This project preserves fixed-target forecasting workflows, simulated outages and paired actual-label loss analyses. Repeated targets and correlated origins stay visible in the interpretation; they are not extra independent samples. The outage scenarios are simulations rather than observations of real infrastructure failure.

**Research direction:** Trustworthy AI

Public source ETT and forecasting inputs; simulated outages do not represent observed infrastructure failures.

## Scope

Repeated targets and correlated origins are not independent replications; simulated outages are not observed infrastructure failures.

Existing source was organized for publication; scientific experiments and tests were not rerun. Cover art is conceptual and does not depict measured results.
