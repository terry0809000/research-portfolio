# Dynamic major adverse kidney-event prediction in T2DM and CKD

![Conceptual scientific cover](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/terry0809000/t2dm-ckd-dynamic-kidney-risk/main/assets/project-cover.png)

Kidney risk unfolds over time, and the population still eligible for prediction changes with it. I wanted to make those moving parts visible rather than hide them inside one headline score.

This project preserves landmark-based modelling for people with diabetes and chronic kidney disease, including endpoint construction, sample-size gates, competing events and frozen external evaluation. Unknown follow-up stays unknown. A common-target comparison is a conditional information analysis, not an early prediction that knows a patient’s future eligibility.

**Research direction:** Prediction Modelling

## Methods and inputs

Landmark MAKE endpoints, gated model development, competing-event sensitivities and frozen external evaluation.

Credentialed MIMIC-IV and eICU source archives; use the portable path adapter where required and preserve frozen evaluation isolation.

## Scope

Unknown renal follow-up remains unknown; common-target comparisons condition on later eligibility and are not early deployable scores.

Existing source was organized for publication; scientific experiments and tests were not rerun. Cover art is conceptual and does not depict measured results.
