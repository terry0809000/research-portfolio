# Laboratory documentation counts and retrospective recording rules

![Conceptual scientific cover](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/terry0809000/icu-documentation-counts/main/assets/project-cover.png)

A count looks simple until we ask what was counted. Repeated laboratory entries, revised results and availability times can change the story a retrospective clinical model tells.

This project preserves a portable, frozen workflow from credentialed raw archives through cohort construction, fixed models and private aggregate candidates. The recording rules are part of the scientific question. Source, installation instructions and schemas are included; data, models and historical evidence are not.

**Research direction:** Health Informatics

## Methods and inputs

First-availability lab recording rules, portable raw-archive cohort construction and fixed private aggregate analyses.

Credentialed eICU/MIMIC archives. Follow the original first-availability, split and local disclosure contracts.

## Scope

Recording counts are not independent biological measurements; real archive reproduction and linked disclosure review are separate gates.

Existing source was organized for publication; scientific experiments and tests were not rerun. Cover art is conceptual and does not depict measured results.
