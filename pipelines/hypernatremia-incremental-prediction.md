# Incremental prediction of hypernatremia

![Conceptual scientific cover](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/terry0809000/hypernatremia-incremental-prediction/main/assets/project-cover.png)

A rising sodium concentration can change the course of an ICU stay. I wanted to understand what prediction adds once we account for the measurements and clinical information already available. This project brings together cohort construction, temporal checks, baseline comparisons and saved robustness analyses for that question.

**Research direction:** Prediction Modelling

## Methods and inputs

Sodium cohort construction, temporal checks, baseline comparisons and robustness analyses.

Credentialed MIMIC-IV and eICU; authorized extraction and phenotype rules are retained.

## Scope

Later optimized analyses reuse inspected evaluation populations and are not independent confirmation.

Existing source was organized for publication; scientific experiments and tests were not rerun. Cover art is conceptual and does not depict measured results.
