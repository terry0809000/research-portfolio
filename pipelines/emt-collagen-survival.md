# EMT and collagen in colon-cancer survival

![Conceptual scientific cover](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/terry0809000/emt-collagen-survival/main/assets/project-cover.png)

A molecular signature can capture an appealing biological story without adding useful predictive information. I compare an EMT score with a compact collagen proxy, asking what survives a move from development data to an external colon-cancer cohort.

The source separates raw-data preprocessing from score-based model fitting and prediction reconstruction. It retains the original failed source requirements and the exploratory status of post hoc diagnostics.

**Research direction:** Prediction Modelling

**Also:** AI for Health

## The question

How do EMT and a three-gene collagen proxy compare for survival prediction in an external colon-cancer cohort?

## Methods and inputs

Cohort-specific preprocessing, penalized survival models, fixed-score reconstruction and paired uncertainty.

Acquire public GEO cohorts and MSigDB inputs separately under their source terms.

[Open the source and pipeline →](https://github.com/terry0809000/emt-collagen-survival)

## Evidence boundary

An exploratory external-cohort comparison; a small uncertain concordance difference does not establish superiority, equivalence or clinical utility.

Existing study source was organized for this portfolio; scientific experiments and tests were not rerun. The AI-generated cover is conceptual raster artwork.
