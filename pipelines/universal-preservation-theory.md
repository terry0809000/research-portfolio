# Universal preservation under stochastic transformations

![Conceptual scientific cover](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/terry0809000/universal-preservation-theory/main/assets/project-cover.png)

More information can make a decision problem surprisingly complicated. I wanted to understand when a shared observation preserves the order between two prior distributions—and when apparently reassuring local checks miss a counterexample.

This repository contains finite-alphabet calculations, LP candidate searches and exact rational certificate checks. Numerical enumeration supports the recorded examples; it is not, by itself, a proof of a universal theorem or evidence of a clinical effect.

**Research direction:** Trustworthy AI
